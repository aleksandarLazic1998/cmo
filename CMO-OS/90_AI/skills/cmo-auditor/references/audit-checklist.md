# Audit Checklist

- Baseline preserved and scope authorized.
- Canonical domain correct; archive/templates are not active authority.
- FACT, INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION, UNKNOWN and FIXTURE are separated.
- Every material number has source, period, unit, grain and formula.
- KPI numerator/denominator use compatible periods and populations.
- Financial analysis distinguishes revenue, gross profit, margin, cash and ROI.
- Research uses appropriate sources and records limitations.
- External output records system, extraction time, query/method and quality.
- `EXECUTE` action had explicit approval.
- No personal state outside Learning Progress.
- No plugin became a parallel source of truth.
- Changelog describes only completed state.
