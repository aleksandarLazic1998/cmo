# Figma Integration Contract

status: active  
default_action: READ

## Allowed without additional business approval

- Read design context, screenshots, metadata, variables, components and libraries.
- Analyze an existing design or diagram.

## Explicit approval required

- Create a new file, design, library or diagram.
- Upload assets, publish mappings or otherwise modify shared Figma state.

## Required evidence

Record file URL/key, node ID, retrieval date, relevant version/context and intended canonical CMO record. Figma is a design artifact system, not the owner of business facts, KPI definitions or decisions.
