## Central thesis

`[FACT — source claim]` Startup je human institution koja stvara novi proizvod/uslugu pod ekstremnom neizvesnošću. Napredak nije količina izgrađenih features-a nego empirijski validated learning o tome da li postoji održiv business. Tim zato pretvara leap-of-faith assumptions u eksperimente, prolazi Build–Measure–Learn loop, meri cohort/actionable metrics i odlučuje da pivotira ili istraje.

`[INFERENCE]` CMO OS zadržava `governed hypothesis portfolio`: svaka inovacija ima decision, falsifiable assumption, evidence threshold, minimum risk-adjusted test, measurement/causal design, financial and stakeholder guardrails, authority, stop/pivot/persevere rule i retrievable result. Brzina učenja je vredna samo kada smanjuje decision uncertainty bez prebacivanja rizika na kupce, zaposlene ili javnost.

## Complete Lean Startup model and corrections

### 1. Extreme uncertainty changes the management system

`[FACT — source content]` Autor startup definiše po extreme uncertainty, ne po veličini, starosti ili sektoru; isti problem može postojati u velikoj kompaniji, vladi ili nonprofit-u (pp. 20–40).

`[INFERENCE]` Definicija je korisna za routing. Work item se ne proglašava startup-om radi izbegavanja accountability-ja: mora navesti šta je stvarno nepoznato, šta je stabilno/poznato, koji standard controls i dalje važe i kada prelazi iz discovery u repeatable execution.

### 2. Vision, strategy and product change at different rates

`[FACT — source content]` Vision je relativno stabilan; strategy obuhvata business model, customer, partners/competitors i roadmap, a product se najčešće menja kroz optimization; pivot menja fundamental strategy hypothesis (pp. 21–29, 136–162).

`[INFERENCE]` „Vision rarely changes“ nije dozvola za dogmu. Vision se ponovo otvara ako krši prava, nema održivu vrednost, traži obmanu ili material evidence opovrgava premise. Product, strategy i vision moraju imati odvojene review/authority gates.

### 3. Validated learning is decision evidence

`[FACT — source content]` Learning dobija status progress-a tek kada nova verzija hipoteze promeni relevantne customer metrics, a ne kada tim ispriča ubedljivu priču posle neuspeha (pp. 41–57).

`[INFERENCE]` Validated learning nije broj testova ni pozitivno pomeranje bilo kog KPI-ja. Potreban je pre-test decision contract: hypothesis, population, expected direction/magnitude, primary outcome, guardrails, observation window, analysis, evidence threshold i action on positive/negative/inconclusive result.

### 4. Build–Measure–Learn plans backwards

`[FACT — source content]` Aktivnosti se izvršavaju Build→Measure→Learn, ali planiranje ide unazad: od onoga što treba naučiti, preko evidence-a koji to pokazuje, do najmanjeg proizvoda/testa koji taj evidence proizvodi (pp. 73–76).

`[INFERENCE]` „Learn“ mora značiti smanjenje specifične decision uncertainty. Ako rezultat ne može promeniti decision, test je activity theater. Ako measurement nije pouzdan ili action nije unapred određen, loop proizvodi narativ, ne kontrolu.

### 5. Leap-of-faith assumptions and fragile-risk order

`[FACT — source content]` Najvažnije neproverene premise business plan-a su leap-of-faith assumptions, naročito value i growth hypotheses; analogs/antilogs pomažu da se izlože skrivena pitanja (pp. 58–87).

`[INFERENCE]` Prvo se testira assumption sa najvećim `material loss × uncertainty × irreversibility`, pod uslovom da je test etičan i izvodljiv. Value/growth nisu jedine premise: delivery, unit economics, payment, capacity, channel, safety, legal, privacy/security, accessibility, supplier i financing assumption mogu biti ranije smrtonosni.

### 6. Customer contact and behavior are complementary evidence

`[FACT — source content]` Genchi gembutsu i „get out of the building“ traže firsthand customer/problem observation; customer archetype ostaje provisional, a observed behavior nadjačava hipotetičko mišljenje u eksperimentu (pp. 58–87).

`[INFERENCE]` Behavior nije automatski preference, welfare ili causality. Interviews objašnjavaju context/mechanism, behavioral data pokazuje šta se desilo, a experiment pokušava da izoluje šta je promena izazvala. Stated needs se ne odbacuju zato što kupac „ne zna šta želi“; contradiction je signal za dalju proveru.

### 7. MVP is a minimum risk-adjusted evidence vehicle

`[FACT — source content]` MVP je najbrži način da se kroz ceo feedback loop testira fundamental business hypothesis; može biti smoke test, video, concierge, Wizard-of-Oz ili functioning early product (pp. 88–106).

`[INFERENCE]` „Minimum“ se odnosi na nepotreban scope, ne na minimum safety/quality/rights. MVP pre release-a mora zadovoljiti non-negotiable safety, security, privacy, accessibility, legal/regulatory, truthful-offer, consent, data and customer-support floor. Material deception, fake availability, unsafe prototype i nezaštićena vulnerable population nisu legitimni learning shortcuts.

### 8. Early adopter evidence does not authorize mass rollout

`[FACT — source content]` Early adopters mogu tolerisati nepotpun proizvod i pomoći učenju pre mainstream market-a; knjiga koristi tvrdnju o „80 percent solution“ i više historical cases (pp. 88–106).

`[INFERENCE]` Tolerancija nije univerzalna osobina niti pristanak na rizik. Early-adopter selection može dati false positive za mainstream usability, trust, willingness-to-pay, accessibility i support burden. Transfer zahteva segment identity, exposure, actual commitment, outcome, harm/complaint i representativeness limits.

### 9. Baseline, tuning and pivot are learning milestones

`[FACT — source content]` Innovation accounting ima tri koraka: MVP baseline, pokušaji da se causal drivers pomere prema sustainable ideal-u, zatim pivot/persevere decision (pp. 107–120).

`[INFERENCE]` Innovation accounting dopunjuje, ne zamenjuje financial accounting. Ideal model mora povezati customer behavior sa fulfilled revenue, contribution/gross profit, CAC/payback, retention, cash/working capital, capacity, obligations i downside. „Moving toward ideal“ nije dokaz ako je ideal nevalidan.

### 10. Cohorts and controlled comparisons defeat gross-number illusion

`[FACT — source content]` Cumulative users/revenue mogu rasti dok yield novih customer cohorts stagnira. Cohort analysis, funnel flow i simultaneous A/B comparisons pomažu da se promena odvoji od legacy mix-a i general growth-a (pp. 107–135).

`[INFERENCE]` Cohort trend je descriptive dok ne isključi mix, seasonality, channel, price, exposure i other changes. A/B causal claim zahteva random/defensible assignment, sample/power, pre-specified primary outcome, concurrent control, stable instrumentation, treatment integrity, multiple-testing/peeking control, uncertainty interval i interference/novelty check.

### 11. Actionable, accessible and auditable metrics need governance

`[FACT — source content]` Three A's traže clear cause/effect, understandable people-based units, široko dostupne reports i proveru against underlying/customer reality (pp. 121–135).

`[INFERENCE]` `Actionable` se ne proglašava bez causal design-a; simple report ne sme skrivati uncertainty. `Accessible` znači dostupno ovlašćenim decision roles uz context, ne svim zaposlenima po default-u. `Auditable` znači lineage, definitions, assignment/exposure, raw-event reconciliation i controlled spot check — ne neovlašćeno kontaktiranje ili identifikovanje kupaca.

### 12. Pivot is a new hypothesis, not random change

`[FACT — source content]` Pivot zadržava jedan oslonac u prethodnom učenju i menja fundamental product, customer, need, platform, architecture, value-capture, growth-engine, channel ili technology hypothesis (pp. 136–162).

`[INFERENCE]` Taxonomy opisuje tip promene, ne dokazuje izbor. Pivot dobija do-nothing/other-option comparison, retained/discarded evidence, migration/customer obligation plan, cost/runway, new risks, stop rule i approval. Sunk cost se ne vraća, ali remaining asset, people impact i contractual duty se ne brišu.

### 13. Runway remains cash, time and option capacity

`[FACT — source content]` Autor predlaže da se runway posmatra kao broj preostalih pivots-a, ne samo cash/burn months (pp. 145–149).

`[INFERENCE]` To je koristan option-capacity lens, ali nije finansijska definicija runway-a. CMO OS prati cash floor, burn, collection/payment timing, liabilities, funding access, lead time do evidence-a i broj feasible decision cycles. Jeftin/brz pivot nije opcija ako ne ostavlja vreme/cash za isporuku i obligations.

### 14. Small batches reduce evidence latency under conditions

`[FACT — source content]` Small batch/single-piece flow ranije otkriva defect i customer rejection, smanjuje WIP/rework i ubrzava feedback; hypothesis pull određuje šta product team radi (pp. 167–183).

`[INFERENCE]` Manji batch nije automatski optimalan. Setup/transaction cost, statistical sample need, physical minimum lot, supplier economics, regulated validation, release risk i cognitive/context-switch cost mogu zahtevati veći batch. Cilj je najmanji risk-adjusted batch koji smanjuje total system cost i evidence delay.

### 15. Continuous deployment requires an immune system

`[FACT — source content]` IMVU example koristi automated tests, business-health monitoring, automatic rollback/block i root-cause work; knjiga izričito kaže da 50 deployments dnevno nije universal target (pp. 169–177).

`[INFERENCE]` Release frequency se izvodi iz blast radius-a i recovery capability-ja. Observability, staged exposure, automated/manual gates, rollback, backups, access, change record, incident response i regulatory approval ostaju obavezni. Eksperiment se ne pušta ako cannot fail safely.

### 16. Growth engines are causal hypotheses, not labels

`[FACT — source content]` Sticky model naglašava retention/churn, viral model referral coefficient, a paid model LTV-versus-CPA margin; autor savetuje focus na jedan dominant engine i očekuje eventualno exhaustion (pp. 184–199).

`[INFERENCE]` Models su korisne decompositions, ali source arithmetic je pojednostavljen. Acquisition i churn rate moraju koristiti kompatibilne bases/periods; viral coefficient iznad 1 nije dovoljan zbog delay-a, saturation-a, fraud-a, harm-a i capacity-ja; LTV>CPA nije dovoljno bez gross contribution, payback, cash timing, refunds/defaults, sales/service cost i overhead/capital. Mixed/multi-sided engines se modeliraju kada stvarno postoje.

### 17. Product/market fit is measured, not declared

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga povezuje product/market fit sa kvantitativnim engine-of-growth signalima i daje retorički rule da firma koja pita da li ga ima verovatno još nije tamo (pp. 196–199).

`[INFERENCE]` Nema universal PMF threshold-a, uključujući viral coefficient `0.9`. Fit se procenjuje po segment/cohort-u kroz repeated value/outcome, retention/repeat, willingness-to-pay/realized price, contribution/cash, scalable acquisition/delivery, complaints/harm i capacity. Fit može nestati promenom tržišta ili kvaliteta.

### 18. Five Whys is a facilitated causal inquiry, not proof

`[FACT — source content]` Five Whys povezuje symptom sa dubljim process/training/management conditions i nalaže proportional preventive investment; Five Blames se sprečava uključivanjem pogođenih i senior sponsorship-om (pp. 200–223).

`[INFERENCE]` Pet pitanja je prompt, ne guaranteed root cause. Stvarni incident može imati više causal/contributing paths, latent conditions i external dependencies. Izjava da je u korenu svakog technical problem-a human problem je preširoka i može vratiti blame. Potrebni su timeline, evidence, causal map, controls, responsible owners, due dates i verification da action smanjuje recurrence/harm.

### 19. Adaptive process protects speed and people

`[FACT — source content]` Autor odbacuje trade-off kvaliteta za brzinu, opisuje andon/stop-the-line, incremental training/process investment i cross-functional ownership (pp. 200–223).

`[INFERENCE]` `First mistake tolerated, same mistake never twice` nije realan just-culture rule. Recurrence se uči po mechanism-u i severity-ju; deliberate/reckless conduct se odvaja od human error i system design-a. Psychological safety, workload, accessibility, expertise i lawful accountability moraju ostati prisutni.

### 20. Innovation sandbox is bounded authority

`[FACT — source content]` Sandbox ograničava scope/customer share/time, dodeljuje end-to-end team, standard metrics, monitoring i catastrophic abort, uz cross-functional autonomy (pp. 224–239).

`[INFERENCE]` Sandbox charter mora dodati decision rights, prohibited zones, eligibility/consent, risk tier, sample/analysis, privacy/security, legal/accessibility/financial approvals, exposure cap, rollback/restitution, customer communication i incident escalation. Autonomy važi unutar mandate-a; ne suspenduje company controls ili stakeholder rights.

### 21. Innovation is a portfolio, not a permanent exception

`[FACT — source content]` Established company istovremeno vodi discovery, growth, optimization i legacy/efficiency work; teams/skills i management system se menjaju kroz faze (pp. 224–239).

`[INFERENCE]` Portfolio razdvaja uncertainty, horizon, capital, metric i governance. Discovery se ne finansira bez review-a zauvek, a core operations se ne tretiraju kao neinovativan trošak. Handoff između faza čuva evidence, customer promises, knowledge, controls i role choice/transition support.

### 22. The method must itself be testable

`[FACT — source content]` Epilogue upozorava da MVP/pivot mogu postati pseudoscience, priznaje da innovation accounting možda nije najbolji metod i poziva na uporedni research (pp. 240–249).

`[INFERENCE]` Ovo je ključna anti-dogma kontrola. Lean Startup label nikada nije evidence. CMO OS meri da li metod smanjuje decision cycle/cost i poboljšava valid, fulfilled, profitable customer outcome uz manje harm-a od realnih alternativa.

## Sherlock test — limitations and failure modes

1. **Success can still be mythologized.** Knjiga kritikuje founder myth, ali dominantno uči kroz naknadno izabrane successful narratives.
2. **Science-label inflation.** Hypothesis/test vocabulary ne garantuje valid design, sample, causal inference ili reproducibility.
3. **Test-volume gaming.** Broj testova može postati vanity metric; multiple testing i low-value ideas proizvode false discoveries i opportunity cost.
4. **Actionable-causality overclaim.** Cohort report i correlated movement sami ne dokazuju cause/effect.
5. **A/B fragility.** Underpowered sample, peeking, unplanned segmentation, multiple outcomes, novelty, spillover i broken assignment mogu preokrenuti zaključak.
6. **Metric optimization harm.** Conversion, time, retention ili referral mogu rasti kroz dark patterns, addiction, lock-in, spam ili customer harm.
7. **Privacy dismissed as friction.** Auditability ne nadjačava minimization, consent, confidentiality, security i purpose limitation.
8. **Unsafe MVP.** Low quality može ugroziti safety, finance, health, access, data, rights ili trust pre nego što tim „nauči“.
9. **Wizard-of-Oz deception.** Skrivena ljudska obrada može obmanuti, izložiti sensitive data ili stvoriti capacity/economic false positive.
10. **Smoke-test misrepresentation.** Preorder/landing page mora jasno opisati status, price/terms, delivery uncertainty i refund/cancel path.
11. **Early-adopter bias.** Niche tolerance ne dokazuje mainstream fit, compliance, accessibility ili scalable support.
12. **False negative.** Premali/loš MVP, pogrešan channel/segment ili unreliable measurement može odbaciti dobru premise-u.
13. **Learning excuse reborn.** Tim može naknadno redefinisati šta je „naučio“ ako decision/action nije precommit-ovan.
14. **Infinite iteration.** Unapred obećanje da se „ne odustaje“ posle MVP-a može pretvoriti flexibility u sunk-cost persistence.
15. **Pivot theater.** Česte promene bez retained evidence-a, economics-a i migration discipline troše runway pod modernim nazivom.
16. **Runway metaphor loss.** Broj pivots-a ne plaća payroll, tax, supplier, refund ili debt i ne zamenjuje cash forecast.
17. **Planning straw man.** Scenario planning, irreversible-risk analysis, compliance design i capacity/financial planning ostaju vredni pod neizvesnošću.
18. **Behavior-is-value fallacy.** Usage/attention nije nužno customer welfare, ethical value ili willingness to fund sustainable economics.
19. **Growth-source overreach.** New customers ne dolaze samo iz past-customer actions; distribution, partnerships, sales, market change i investment mogu biti causal.
20. **Sticky arithmetic mismatch.** Acquisition i churn percentages zahtevaju isti population/time basis; prosto oduzimanje može biti pogrešno.
21. **Viral threshold myth.** Coefficient >1 ne garantuje success, a <1 ne znači absence of sustainable mixed growth.
22. **Paid-engine incompleteness.** LTV–CPA spread ignoriše payback timing, overhead, service/capacity, bad debt/refunds, capital i risk.
23. **One-engine dogma.** Focus pomaže, ali multi-sided/mixed model se ne sme iskriviti da bi odgovarao taksonomiji.
24. **PMF rhetoric.** „If you are asking, you are not there“ i `0.9` viral threshold nisu decision-grade pravila.
25. **Small-batch absolutism.** Setup, validation, sample, supplier i physical constraints mogu učiniti ultra-small batch skupljim ili rizičnijim.
26. **Continuous-deployment overtransfer.** Mission-critical/regulisani systems traže risk-based gates, evidence, approvals i controlled rollback.
27. **Root-cause singularity.** Five Whys može prerano stati na jednom story-ju i propustiti paralelne causes, conditions ili control failures.
28. **Human-root blame.** „Every technical problem is human“ može personalizovati system/external failure i potisnuti just culture.
29. **Never-twice impossibility.** Recurrence nije automatski misconduct; rule može podstaći hiding i underreporting.
30. **Sandbox statistical certainty.** „Metric moves or not“ bez uncertainty/power može doneti pogrešan abort/scale decision.
31. **Autonomy without authority boundary.** Cross-functional speed ne ukida legal, financial, security, privacy, safety ili customer approvals.
32. **Incentive gaming.** Personal/equity stake može povećati metric manipulation, risk-taking i suppression of negative evidence.
33. **Public-service experimentation risk.** Geographic/use-case tests mogu nepravedno uskratiti uslugu; equity, due process i accessibility su mandatory.
34. **Education/civic experimentation risk.** Student outcomes i political participation nisu neutralni product metrics; rights and public-interest governance apply.
35. **NPS overclaim.** NPS nije sam po sebi stable, actionable ili causal; sample, response bias, distribution, drivers i paired outcomes su potrebni.
36. **Commercial conflict.** Author relationships sa case companies povećavaju potrebu za independent corroboration i negative-case evidence-om.

- Innovation accounting je decision-learning layer, ne P&L/balance sheet/cash-flow replacement.
- LTV, CPA, revenue, runway i growth engine se reconciles sa gross contribution, cash timing, working capital, debt, tax, obligations, reserve i downside.
- Behavioral budget/envelope može ograničiti experiment spend, ali ne dokazuje experiment economics.

### With BK-008 — High Output Management

- Build–Measure–Learn i small batches potvrđuju total-system output, indicator pairing, manager leverage i process feedback.
- Cross-functional learning ne poništava role clarity; manager mora obezbediti authority, capability, quality gates i paired customer/financial/people/risk metrics.

### With BK-004 and BK-005 — Built to Sell and The E-Myth Revisited

- Repeatable process nije fiksna dogma; standard postaje current best tested default sa feedback/exception/retirement rule-om.
- Founder-independent system mora sačuvati discovery capability, ne samo replicirati stari offer.

### With BK-015 — Who

- Innovation role scorecard meri quality of decisions and demonstrated learning, ne broj shipped features ili tests.
- Cross-functional autonomy zahteva explicit mission, outcomes, resources, decision rights, dependencies i ethical/legal guardrails; „entrepreneur type“ nije hiring proxy.

## Innovation experiment protocol

### Problem

Imenuj decision i uncertainty, pogođeni customer/stakeholders, owner/decider, deadline, material downside i zašto standard execution/analysis nije dovoljan.

### Data

Zabeleži current baseline/cohort, direct customer/problem evidence, business model/financial bridge, relevant prior tests, population/exposure, data source/quality, legal/privacy/security/accessibility/safety constraints, capacity i competing explanations. Obeleži `[FACT]`, `[INFERENCE]`, `[ASSUMPTION]`, `[UNKNOWN]`.

### Analysis

1. Rangiraj assumptions po material loss-u, uncertainty-ju i irreversibility-ju.
2. Napiši falsifiable prediction i alternative explanation.
3. Definiši primary outcome, guardrails, sample/window, assignment/comparison i uncertainty treatment pre testa.
4. Dizajniraj minimum risk-adjusted evidence vehicle i rollback/restitution.
5. Reconcile customer behavior sa fulfilled economics, cash, capacity i obligations.

### Options

Poredi no-test/no-change, qualitative discovery, smoke/prototype, concierge/Wizard, controlled MVP/sandbox, staged rollout i full launch. Za svaku navedi evidence gain, cost, cycle time, bias, harm, reversibility i authorization.

### Recommendation

Izaberi najmanji autorizovan test koji može promeniti odluku i bezbedno opovrgnuti fragile assumption. Ako mandatory safety/legal/privacy/accounting evidence ili rollback nedostaje, recommendation je `STOP` ili redesign, ne „ship faster“.

### Cost

Build/measurement, recruitment, incentives, support, opportunity cost, delay, data/security/compliance, refunds/remediation, technical debt, staff workload, customer trust, working capital i follow-on scale cost.

### Expected result

Navedi expected effect sa intervalom i decision table: positive, negative, mixed/guardrail breach i inconclusive. Learning postoji tek kada rezultat ažurira belief/model i aktivira predefinisanu action.

### KPI

Primary behavior/outcome; cohort activation/retention/repeat; realized price/contribution; CAC/payback/cash; fulfilled quality/customer result; complaints/refunds/harm; accessibility/privacy/security/safety; experiment exposure/integrity; cycle time/WIP; capacity/workload; stop/pivot/scale trigger. Svaki KPI ima definition, numerator/denominator, cohort/period, source, owner i audit lineage.

## Canonical exclusions

- Lean Startup termin nije dokaz da je projekat naučan, brz, bezbedan ili profitabilan.
- Validated learning i innovation accounting ne zamenjuju customer value, financial accounting, cash forecast, delivery, legal/compliance i management accountability.
- MVP ne znači low-quality-by-default i ne suspenduje safety, security, privacy, accessibility, consent, truthfulness ili support.
- Customer use, attention, conversion, retention, referral, NPS, LTV, CPA, viral coefficient ili test count nisu samostalni success/PMF rules.
- Cohort trend nije automatski causality; A/B label nije valid experiment bez design/integrity/uncertainty checks.
- Autorovi 2004–2011 company numbers, $ amounts, valuations, adoption claims i outcomes nisu current facts ili Tehnocentar benchmarks.
- One-engine focus, viral `0.9/1.0`, `80 percent solution`, fixed pivot cadence, Five Whys count i sandbox size nisu universal standards.
- Customer/public/employee/student/citizen experimentation ne zaobilazi rights, fairness, informed governance ili current law.
- „Every technical problem is human“, „never same mistake twice“ i unrestricted team autonomy nisu CMO OS people/incident policy.
- Book community, conference, blogs, author cases i disclosed investments remain source context, not authority.
