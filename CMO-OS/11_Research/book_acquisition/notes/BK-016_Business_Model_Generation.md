## Central thesis

`[FACT — source claim]` Poslovni model opisuje logiku kako organizacija stvara, isporučuje i zahvata vrednost. Devet međuzavisnih blokova Canvas-a daju zajednički vizuelni jezik; patterns daju inspiraciju; customer insight, ideation, visualization, prototyping, storytelling and scenarios stvaraju opcije; environment, SWOT, Blue Ocean and portfolio choices proveravaju strateški kontekst; five-phase process vodi od mobilizacije do kontinuiranog upravljanja.

`[INFERENCE]` CMO OS koristi Canvas kao `governed business-model hypothesis graph`. Svaki element ima segment, owner, source, evidence state, economics, capacity, dependency, risk/rights boundary, uncertainty i next test. Model nije odobren zato što je potpun na posteru; odobren je tek kada njegove kritične veze mogu da prežive falsification, financial reconciliation i operational reality.

## Complete model and CMO OS corrections

### 1. A business model is a system, not nine independent boxes

`[FACT — source content]` Devet blokova pokriva Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partnerships and Cost Structure; knjiga ih tretira kao povezanu celinu (pp. 20–55).

`[INFERENCE]` Svaka strelica između blokova je hipoteza. Na primer, VP→segment traži problem/value evidence; segment→channel traži reach/conversion evidence; revenue→cost traži unit economics, timing and cash; activities/resources→delivery traže capacity, quality and control evidence. Canvas bez veza i stanja dokaza je inventar imenica, ne model.

### 2. Segment choice is deliberate exclusion plus economic fit

`[FACT — source content]` Segmenti se razdvajaju kada imaju različite potrebe, kanale, odnose, profitabilnost ili willingness to pay; organizacija bira koga služi i koga ignoriše (pp. 26–27).

`[INFERENCE]` Segment mora imati observable definition i evidence o jobs/pains/outcomes, reachability, conversion, realized price, gross margin, CAC/payback, retention, support/capacity burden, payment timing, concentration and harm risk. „Mass“, „niche“ ili „B2B“ nisu dovoljna segmentacija.

### 3. Value Proposition is an outcome contract, not a feature list

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga grupiše izvore vrednosti: newness, performance, customization, job completion, design, status, price, cost/risk reduction, accessibility and convenience (pp. 28–31).

`[INFERENCE]` Svaki benefit se prevodi u specific customer outcome, alternative, proof, delivery condition, time-to-value, exclusions, price and remedy. Benefit koji ne može biti dokaziv ili isporučen pod definisanim uslovima ostaje claim; status, scarcity, lock-in and convenience ne smeju prikriti obmanu ili switching harm.

### 4. Channel is a five-phase evidence chain

`[FACT — source content]` Channel pokriva awareness, evaluation, purchase, delivery and after-sales; owned/partner and direct/indirect izbori menjaju reach, margin, control and customer experience (pp. 32–33).

`[INFERENCE]` CMO OS meri transition po fazi, total CAC, contribution, cash timing, data/consent, dependency and recovery. Kanal sa visokim reach-om može biti loš ako ima nizak qualified conversion, skupe povrate, slabu naplatu ili partner concentration.

### 5. Customer Relationship design must preserve agency

`[FACT — source content]` Relationship modeli obuhvataju personal/dedicated assistance, self-service, automation, communities and co-creation, uz acquisition, retention and upsell motive (pp. 34–35).

`[INFERENCE]` Retention i switching cost nisu automatski vrednost. Relationship gate proverava informed choice, cancellation/portability, privacy, accessibility, complaint/remedy, support load and long-term outcome. Community/co-creation traže governance, attribution, moderation, safety and compensation rules.

### 6. Revenue Streams do not equal profit or cash

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga razdvaja transaction and recurring revenue i opisuje više pricing mechanisms, uz kratku napomenu da troškovi moraju biti oduzeti da bi nastala zarada (pp. 36–39).

`[INFERENCE]` Svaki stream se reconciles sa recognized revenue, discounts/refunds/defaults, COGS, variable support, contribution margin, CAC/payback, receivables, working capital, tax, liabilities, capital needs and cash timing. Recurring revenue nije kvalitet ako recurring obligation, churn, service cost ili deferred delivery unište contribution/cash.

### 7. Resources, activities and partnerships require constraint and control design

`[FACT — source content]` Resources mogu biti physical, intellectual, human and financial; activities su production, problem solving ili platform/network; partnerships služe scale, risk reduction and capability acquisition (pp. 40–45).

`[INFERENCE]` „Key“ znači da gubitak ili degradacija menja system output. Za svaki key element evidentirati capacity, bottleneck, quality/SLA, substitutability, concentration, ownership/IP, data/security, continuity, authority and cost. Outsourcing prenosi execution, ne accountability.

### 8. Cost minimization is not a universal objective

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga razlikuje cost-driven and value-driven modele, ali kaže da troškove prirodno treba minimizovati (pp. 46–47).

`[INFERENCE]` CMO OS odbacuje apsolut. Cilj je optimalan risk-adjusted total cost za obećani outcome. Safety, quality, resilience, privacy/security, accessibility, compliance, fair work and recovery capacity nisu „waste“ samo zato što ne povećavaju trenutni revenue.

### 9. Canvas is a shared language, not strategy itself

`[FACT — source content]` Canvas je zamišljen kao veliki, pomerljiv, grupni artefakt za razumevanje, diskusiju, kreativnost i analizu; business model je blueprint za strategy implementation kroz structure, processes and systems (pp. 20–21, 48–55).

`[INFERENCE]` Model ne daje Rumelt-style diagnosis, guiding policy and coherent action; ne određuje constraint, sequence, trade-off ili resource commitment. Zato Canvas prati strategy kernel, decision log, financial model, operating plan, experiment register and risk/control register.

### 10. Patterns are analogies, not recipes

`[FACT — source content]` Unbundling, Long Tail, Multi-Sided, FREE and Open patterns standardizuju ponavljajuće arrangements i mogu se kombinovati (pp. 56–127).

`[INFERENCE]` Pattern samo predlaže mehanizam za test. Transfer zahteva similarity proof: customer behavior, cost/margin structure, network effects, inventory/distribution economics, regulation, capabilities, competitive response and failure modes. Poznat logo nije evidence da je mehanizam dostupan lokalnom poslovanju.

### 11. Unbundling follows incompatible economics and controls

`[FACT — source content]` Customer relationship, product innovation and infrastructure businesses mogu imati različite scale/scope/speed/culture imperative i konflikte kada su zajedno (pp. 62–71).

`[INFERENCE]` Separation decision koristi conflict, similarity, synergy, risk and control criteria, ali uključuje i customer continuity, transfer pricing, data/IP, brand, regulated accountability, shared services, tax/legal obligations and exit reversibility. Razdvajanje može stvoriti handoff waste i novu koncentraciju.

### 12. Long Tail works only when full niche economics work

`[FACT — source content]` Long Tail agregira mnogo retkih prodaja kada su production, inventory, distribution and search/matching troškovi dovoljno niski; Lulu and LEGO ilustruju mehanizam (pp. 72–81).

`[INFERENCE]` Potrebni su contribution distribution, catalog acquisition/quality/moderation, discoverability, returns/support, supplier/creator fairness, platform take rate and working-capital analysis. Velik katalog može povećati complexity and search cost brže od prihoda.

### 13. Multi-sided platforms need causal network-effect and subsidy evidence

`[FACT — source content]` Vrednost jednoj strani zavisi od prisustva druge; platforma bira koju stranu subvencioniše i kako rešava chicken-and-egg (pp. 82–93).

`[INFERENCE]` „Network effect“ nije sinonim za broj naloga. Meriti same-side and cross-side effect, activation, liquidity/match quality, multi-homing, concentration, fraud/moderation, trust/safety, negative externalities, take-rate elasticity and contribution by side. Subsidy ima stop rule; dominacija ili lock-in nisu customer value.

### 14. FREE and freemium are financed offers, never costless offers

`[FACT — source content]` Free segment finansiraju oglašivači, premium users, follow-up purchases ili veliki pool platiša; ključne metrike su cost-to-serve and conversion, uz fixed/variable cost (pp. 94–113).

`[INFERENCE]` Dodati abuse, moderation, support, infrastructure peaks, privacy/data monetization, fraud, compliance, churn, customer acquisition, premium-service delivery and opportunity cost. „Manje od 10%“ nije universal benchmark. Ako korisnik plaća pažnjom/podacima ili trpi manipulativni lock-in, ponuda nije bez troška za njega.

### 15. Bait-and-hook requires a fairness and interoperability gate

`[FACT — source content]` Jeftin ili subvencionisan početni proizvod generiše ponovljene high-margin purchases, a kontrola lock-in-a je centralna (pp. 104–107).

`[INFERENCE]` Primena zahteva transparentan total cost of ownership, compatible alternatives, cancellation/portability, repair/accessibility, fair terms, no dark patterns and legal/competition review. Profitabilnost zasnovana na skrivenom ili prinudnom lock-in-u nije canonical recommendation.

### 16. Open innovation expands the evidence and IP boundary

`[FACT — source content]` Outside-in unosi tuđe ideas/technology/IP; inside-out monetizuje ili ustupa neiskorišćena interna sredstva; connector platforms smanjuju search cost (pp. 114–127).

`[INFERENCE]` Open ne znači uncontrolled. Potrebni su source/provenance, license/ownership, confidentiality, privacy/security, export/regulatory, conflict, attribution/compensation, acceptance criteria, integration capacity and lifecycle accountability.

### 17. Empathy Map is a hypothesis generator, not customer evidence

`[FACT — source content]` Empathy Map traži šta customer vidi, čuje, misli/oseća, kaže/radi, njegove pains and gains; svrha je dublje razumevanje okruženja, ponašanja, briga and aspirations (pp. 128–139).

`[INFERENCE]` Unutrašnja stanja koja tim popuni bez kupca su `[ASSUMPTION]`, ne insight. Profil mora izbeći stereotypes, protected-attribute inference and fabricated emotion; trianguliše interviews, observation, behavioral data and costly commitments uz consent/privacy. Saying and doing conflict je research question, ne dozvola da tim proglasi kupca neiskrenim.

### 18. Ideation separates divergence from governed convergence

`[FACT — source content]` Tim prvo generiše mnogo opcija, zatim bira criteria i razvija tri do pet prototipa; diversity, immersion, deferred judgment and visual work pomažu (pp. 140–151).

`[INFERENCE]` Defer judgment ne odlaže non-negotiable rights/safety/legal constraints. Convergence criteria uključuju problem evidence, strategic coherence, economics/cash, capacity, reversibility, risk, stakeholder effect, time-to-evidence and opportunity cost. Authority za selection je unapred jasna.

### 19. Visual thinking exposes assumptions but can also conceal them

`[FACT — source content]` Vizuelni artefakti pretvaraju implicitne pretpostavke u shared objects, olakšavaju whole-system view, relationship discussion, play and communication (pp. 152–165).

`[INFERENCE]` Svaki sticky note nosi claim tag, source, owner and uncertainty. Lep raspored, ikona, boja ili ubedljiva priča ne podižu evidence weight. Readability and accessibility standardi važe; artefakt mora imati version history i vezu ka detaljnim dokazima.

### 20. Prototype explores model logic before it earns commitment

`[FACT — source content]` Prototip može biti napkin sketch, elaborated Canvas, business-case spreadsheet ili field test; više varijanti služi inquiry, a ne samo refinement-u omiljene ideje (pp. 166–175).

`[INFERENCE]` Skala prototipa prati risk. Napkin/Canvas proverava coherence, spreadsheet economics, controlled test behavior/delivery. Field test je minimum risk-adjusted evidence vehicle sa consent, safety/privacy/security/accessibility floor, observation plan, rollback and stop criteria.

### 21. Storytelling communicates a model; it does not validate it

`[FACT — source content]` Story može učiniti novi model opipljivim, predstaviti customer/company perspective, provocirati promenu i pomoći internom/eksternom pitch-u (pp. 176–185).

`[INFERENCE]` Story je označen kao `[SCENARIO]`, `[ASSUMPTION]` ili evidence-backed case. Ne sme izmišljenu personu ili budućnost predstaviti kao customer fact; ne sme sakriti downside, uncertainty, excluded stakeholders or economics. Posle priče dolaze evidence table and decision consequences.

### 22. Scenarios are stress tests, not forecasts

`[FACT — source content]` Customer scenarios konkretizuju usage settings; future scenarios kombinuju glavne uncertain drivers da testiraju više mogućih okruženja, bez tvrdnje da predviđaju budućnost (pp. 186–197).

`[INFERENCE]` Scenario set navodi drivers, plausible ranges, source, internal consistency, signposts, trigger thresholds, options, no-regret moves and reversal cost. Dve ose nisu obavezno dovoljne; outlier, regulatory, supplier, cash and stakeholder scenarios se dodaju kada su material.

### 23. Environment scanning bounds the design space

`[FACT — source content]` Market forces, industry forces, key trends and macroeconomic forces oblikuju choices; book asks about customers, entrants, substitutes, value-chain actors, stakeholders, technology, regulation, society, capital and infrastructure (pp. 202–217).

`[INFERENCE]` Skeniranje je dated evidence register, ne wall of trends. Svaki signal ima source quality, recency, direction, magnitude, confidence, mechanism, affected blocks, indicator and decision implication. Current claims require live authoritative sources.

### 24. SWOT must be evidence-weighted

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga kombinuje internal strengths/weaknesses and external opportunities/threats sa svakim Canvas blokom, uz importance and certainty ratings (pp. 218–225).

`[INFERENCE]` SWOT bez source and comparative baseline je opinion inventory. CMO OS dodaje evidence, owner, comparator, magnitude, time horizon, controllability, dependency, leading indicator and action. Certainty nije osećaj; proizlazi iz provenance and validation.

### 25. Eliminate–Reduce–Raise–Create is a model stress tool, not a free lunch

`[FACT — source content]` Blue Ocean lens koristi četiri akcije da istovremeno razmatra customer value and cost effects kroz ceo Canvas (pp. 226–231).

`[INFERENCE]` Diferencijacija i niži cost nisu garantovani. Svaka akcija mora pokazati šta kupac gubi/dobija, change in demand, delivery capacity, full cost, risk, competitor response and cash requirement. „Uncontested market“ ostaje assumption dok behavior and economics ne potvrde.

### 26. Multiple models require explicit portfolio governance

`[FACT — source content]` Similarity, synergy, conflict and risk utiču na integration-versus-separation; odluka može da evoluira i fazno se menja (pp. 232–247).

`[INFERENCE]` Svaki model ima mandate, owner, budget/cash, KPIs/guardrails, shared-service terms, transfer price, brand/data/IP boundaries, decision rights, escalation and stop/integrate/spin criteria. Autonomy bez controls stvara skriven rizik; integration bez zaštite guši novi model.

### 27. Five phases are an iterative governance loop

`[FACT — source content]` Mobilize→Understand→Design→Implement→Manage nije linearan; research and prototyping se vraćaju jedno na drugo, a model se kontinuirano ocenjuje and adapts (pp. 248–267).

`[INFERENCE]` Svaka faza ima entry/exit evidence, decision authority and artifact. Implement nije kraj učenja; Manage vodi model portfolio, environment signals and financial/operational/customer outcomes. „Continuously adapt“ nije dozvola da se success criteria menjaju posle rezultata.

### 28. Beyond-profit models still need value, funding and accountability

`[FACT — source content]` Public/nonprofit/mission models imaju value recipients and funders koji mogu biti različiti; knjiga upozorava na incentive misalignment i predlaže social/environmental positive and negative outcomes (pp. 268–271).

`[INFERENCE]` Donor/funder nije zamena za rights and outcome of recipient. Model uključuje mission/outcome, access/equity, voice/consent, harm, environmental and financial measures, independent oversight and complaint/remedy. Triple bottom line bez metric definition, baseline, attribution and assurance može postati impact theater.

### 29. Business plan expands the model into execution evidence

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga predlaže Team, Business Model, Financial Analysis, External Environment, Implementation Roadmap and Risk Analysis, sa breakeven, scenarios, operating/capital costs, funding and milestones (pp. 268–269).

`[INFERENCE]` Canvas prethodi planu, ali ga ne zamenjuje. Financial model mora imati P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, working capital, unit economics, assumptions/sensitivity and funding covenant; roadmap ima dependencies/capacity/owners; risk analysis ima controls, residual risk and triggers.

### 30. Organization and IT must follow model requirements without erasing controls

`[FACT — source content]` Strategy, Structure, Processes, Rewards and People treba uskladiti sa modelom; applications/data/technology treba uskladiti sa business needs (pp. 270–273).

`[INFERENCE]` Incentives imaju outcome and guardrail pair da spreče gaming. IT alignment uključuje data minimization, access, security/privacy, availability/recovery, architecture lock-in, lineage, cost and technical-debt consequences; poslovna želja ne ukida mandatory controls.

## Evidence quality and currency

- `[FACT]` Knjiga je design handbook ko-kreiran sa 470 practitioners iz 45 zemalja; izvori su management/design literatura, cases, interviews, practitioner comments and author experience.
- `[FACT]` Reference list ne pokazuje controlled comparative validation Business Model Canvas metode niti unbiased sample svih business-model outcomes.
- `[INFERENCE]` Case-heavy positive examples nose selection, survivorship, attribution, hindsight and storyteller bias; često nema counterfactual-a ni contribution of alternative explanations.
- `[FACT]` Brojni case metrics and organizational states su iz približno 2005–2010 perioda i ne mogu se tretirati kao current facts 2026.
- `[FACT]` Knjiga sama prikazuje sopstveni revenue model: paid Hub membership, advance/post-publication sales, customized versions and publisher royalties; authors/producer operate in business-model advisory ecosystem.
- `[INFERENCE]` Sopstveni successful launch i practitioner testimonials nisu independent proof of method efficacy. Oni jesu evidence da je book/community model privukao rane kupce i učesnike u tom istorijskom kontekstu.
- `[UNKNOWN]` Current status, economics and causal outcomes svih named cases bez fresh primary-source verification.
- `[UNKNOWN]` Tehnocentar segmenti, customer problems, alternatives, willingness to pay, channels, unit economics, capacity, risks and portfolio choices dok lokalni evidence ne postoji.

## Sherlock test — limitations and failure modes

| Failure mode | Why it matters | CMO OS control |
|---|---|---|
| Complete-looking Canvas | Empty certainty from filled boxes | Claim/evidence/owner/uncertainty per element and link |
| Static snapshot | Hides timing, sequence and feedback | Versioned model, state transitions, indicators and decision log |
| Revenue without cash | Growth can bankrupt the model | P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and working-capital reconciliation |
| Average segment | Masks profitable/unprofitable cohorts | Cohort contribution, CAC/payback, retention and service burden |
| Value assertion | Feature/benefit is not observed outcome | Alternative, proof, behavior, delivery and remedy evidence |
| Channel reach vanity | Reach can increase loss | Phase conversion, total CAC, returns, contribution and cash |
| Retention through lock-in | Revenue may come from trapped users | Agency, portability, cancellation, fairness and complaint gate |
| Partner dependence | Outsourcing hides concentration and control | SLA, substitution, continuity, data/IP and accountability |
| Cost cutting | Removes safety/resilience/quality | Risk-adjusted total-cost and mandatory-control floor |
| Pattern copying | Famous case does not transfer | Mechanism and context similarity test |
| Long-tail overload | Catalog complexity exceeds niche contribution | Distribution analysis, moderation and discoverability economics |
| Platform user-count illusion | Accounts do not prove liquidity/effect | Cross-side causal metrics, matching, fraud, multi-homing and take rate |
| Free-user explosion | Cost-to-serve and abuse overwhelm payer base | Contribution per side, capacity, abuse/support and subsidy stop rule |
| Bait-and-hook harm | Hidden TCO or coercive switching | Transparent TCO, alternatives, compatibility and legal review |
| Open-innovation leakage | IP/data/confidentiality loss | Provenance, license, security, acceptance and lifecycle governance |
| Empathy fiction | Team stereotypes become customer truth | Tag assumptions and triangulate first-party evidence |
| Brainstorming theatre | Idea volume without decision quality | Predefined convergence criteria and authority |
| Visual persuasion | Aesthetics raise unsupported claim weight | Evidence links, accessible labels and uncertainty display |
| Prototype harm | Field test exposes people/systems | Risk tier, consent, guardrails, rollback and incident authority |
| Story manipulation | Emotion suppresses downside | Scenario label, counter-story, evidence and full consequence table |
| Scenario certainty | Plausible story becomes forecast | Drivers, signposts, triggers, ranges and options |
| Trend wall | Unverified/currentness-free observations | Source, recency, magnitude, mechanism and decision implication |
| SWOT opinion | Self-rating lacks comparator/evidence | Evidence-weighted score and external baseline |
| Blue-ocean optimism | Value-up/cost-down assumed | Demand, full cost, capability, response and cash test |
| Autonomy conflict | New model creates ungoverned exposure | Mandate, budget, shared-service rules and escalation |
| Integration suffocation | Core incentives kill exploration | Protected authority, stage funding and separate metrics |
| Endless adaptation | Team moves goalposts | Precommitted success/stop/change rules |
| Portfolio theatre | Experiments persist without resource discipline | Model-level P&L/cash, review cadence and kill/scale thresholds |
| Mission displacement | Funder preferences override recipients | Recipient outcome/rights, governance and remedy |
| Incentive gaming | Rewards optimize local metric | Paired outcome/guardrail metrics and cross-functional review |
| Historical-case literalism | Old facts become present strategy | Treat as mechanism illustration; refresh with primary sources |

## Business-model decision protocol

### Problem

Define material decision, current model version, desired customer/business/stakeholder outcome and why a redesign is needed now.

### Data

For all nine blocks and material links record `[FACT]`, `[INFERENCE]`, `[ASSUMPTION]` and `[UNKNOWN]`; add source, recency, owner, confidence, economics, capacity, risk/rights and dependency evidence.

### Analysis

1. Draw current model and its causal/dependency links.
2. Identify bottleneck and most fragile material assumptions.
3. Reconcile customer outcome, system output, P&L, balance sheet, cash and obligations.
4. Scan market/industry/trend/macro signals with current authoritative evidence.
5. Stress current and candidate models under downside scenarios and stakeholder harms.

### Options

Keep at least three materially different model options plus `do nothing/repair current model`; show which blocks and links change, not merely a renamed VP.

### Recommendation

Select the option with strongest evidence-adjusted profitable growth and manageable reversible downside. State rejected options and the evidence that would reopen them.

### Cost

Include build/test/transition cost, working capital, opportunity cost, capability and support load, partner/technology dependency, control cost and expected downside.

### Expected result

State customer outcome, contribution/cash, strategic learning, operating capability and stakeholder effect with time horizon and confidence.

### KPI

Use outcome/guardrail pairs: customer result and harm/complaint; conversion and total CAC; revenue and contribution/cash; speed and quality; utilization and capacity/safety; learning and decision changed. Add source, owner, cadence and stop/pivot/scale threshold.

## Canonical exclusions

- No filled Canvas is proof of market demand, product-market fit, strategy or viability.
- No historical company case is a current fact or transferable prescription without refreshed primary evidence.
- No user count proves network effect; no recurring revenue proves profit/cash; no high switching cost proves customer value.
- No Empathy Map entry about thought/feeling becomes customer fact without first-party evidence.
- No prototype, story, scenario or workshop output may bypass safety, rights, legal, finance or authority controls.
- No stakeholder, funder, donor, partner, consultant or plugin output becomes Tehnocentar canonical truth without validation.
