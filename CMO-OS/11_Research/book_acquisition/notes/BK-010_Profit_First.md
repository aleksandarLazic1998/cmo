## Central thesis

`[FACT — source claim]` Ljudi prilagođavaju trošenje raspoloživom novcu. Zato knjiga nalaže da se svaki cash inflow unapred podeli na namenski odvojene bank accounts, da se Profit, Owner's Pay i Tax izdvoje pre Operating Expenses i da se raspodela ponavlja u fiksnom ritmu.

`[INFERENCE]` Korisno jezgro nije nova formula profita nego `behavioral treasury overlay`: fizička ili ledger segregacija, precommitment, ograničenje vidljivog/spendable cash-a, named purpose, redovan review i controlled release. Overlay može smanjiti impulsivno trošenje, ali ne proizvodi računovodstveni ili ekonomski profit i ne sme zameniti financial statements, cash forecast, liabilities, controls ili professional authority.

## Complete model and CMO OS correction

### 1. The formula flip is behavioral, not mathematical

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga menja redosled `Sales − Expenses = Profit` u `Sales − Profit = Expenses` da bi profit postao prvi allocation (pp. 24–47).

`[INFERENCE]` Jednačine su algebraički ekvivalentne. Efekat može doći samo iz novog decision rule-a i friction-a: manje cash-a je dostupno za OpEx. CMO OS ga naziva cash-allocation control, ne novim profit modelom.

### 2. Four behavioral principles

`[FACT — source content]` Metoda koristi manje „tanjire“, redosled serving-a, uklanjanje iskušenja i enforcement rhythm: više namenski odvojenih računa, Profit/Tax first, udaljeni računi bez lake potrošnje i allocation/payment cadence (pp. 24–47).

`[INFERENCE]` Svaki princip može biti koristan samo ako named-account balance ima usaglašenu ledger definiciju, owner, allowed use, approval right, reconciliation i exception protocol. Behavioral friction ne sme blokirati ovlašćeni monitoring ili hitno zakonito plaćanje.

### 3. Basic account architecture

`[FACT — source content]` Osnovni računi su Income, Profit, Owner's Pay, Tax i Operating Expenses; Profit i Tax se dodatno odvajaju kod druge banke radi smanjenja iskušenja (pp. 39–76).

`[INFERENCE]` Broj fizičkih računa nije canonical. Isto načelo se može ostvariti treasury subaccounts-ima ili kontrolisanim sub-ledger-om kada daje jednaku segregaciju i manji fee/admin/cyber/reconciliation risk. Segregation of duties, role-based access, dual approval i bank reconciliation imaju prednost nad „skrivanjem“ novca.

### 4. Allocation rhythm

`[FACT — source content]` Autor predlaže allocation i payment rhythm 10. i 25. dana, quarterly review i 50% quarterly Profit distribution dok druga polovina ostaje kao reserve (pp. 39–47, 77–97).

`[INFERENCE]` Ritam je default hypothesis, ne univerzalni kalendar. Contractual due dates, payroll, tax, supplier terms, discounts, customer obligations, liquidity i local law određuju stvarni schedule. Distribution prolazi capital-allocation gate: solvency/liquidity, working capital, seasonality, debt/covenants, capex, tax, ownership/fiduciary rights i downside reserve.

### 5. Start-small transition

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga preporučuje da se krene od current allocation percentage-a, doda približno jedan procentni poen i postepeno približava Target Allocation Percentages tokom quarterly review-a (pp. 48–97).

`[INFERENCE]` Mala promena i feedback smanjuju implementation shock. Međutim, source TAP bands nisu validirani benchmark-i. Cilj se izvodi iz stvarnih unit economics, operating model-a, owner replacement cost-a, tax treatment-a, obligations-a, capacity-ja i stress forecast-a.

### 6. `Real Revenue` is a nonstandard management construct

`[FACT — source content]` Instant Assessment definiše Real Revenue kao sales umanjen za materials i subcontractors, ali ne za employee labor (pp. 48–62).

`[INFERENCE]` To nije automatski recognized revenue, gross profit ili contribution margin. Neujednačeno isključivanje subcontractor-a, a zadržavanje employee labor-a može iskriviti poređenje make-versus-buy modela i scale bands. CMO OS koristi reconciled accounting revenue i zasebno definisanu contribution/gross-profit metriku.

### 7. Cash allocation is not profit recognition

`[FACT — source claim]` Knjiga tvrdi da business postaje trajno profitabilan od sledećeg deposit-a kada se Profit percentage prvi izdvoji (pp. 63–76).

`[INFERENCE]` Tvrdnja je netačna ako se čita računovodstveno ili ekonomski. Cash može poticati iz debt-a, customer advances-a, sales tax-a, owner capital-a, asset sale-a ili neispunjenih obligations-a. Premeštanje cash-a samo po sebi ne menja recognized revenue, expenses, assets, liabilities ili sustainable unit economics.

### 8. Profit distribution and reserve are different decisions

`[FACT — source content]` Polovina quarterly Profit account-a se deli vlasniku, druga polovina se zadržava; kasnije se reserve povezuje sa oko tri meseca i mogućim reinvestiranjem (pp. 77–97, 152–168).

`[INFERENCE]` Knjiga povremeno meša profit allocation, owner reward, rainy-day reserve i reinvestment capital. CMO OS ih odvaja: earned distributable profit, statutory/tax reserve, operating liquidity floor, contingency reserve i approved growth capital imaju različite purpose, authority i release gates.

### 9. Debt payoff requires economic and legal prioritization

`[FACT — source content]` Chapter Six kombinuje expense cuts, lifestyle lock i behavioral debt snowball, uz održavanje profit rituala (pp. 98–120).

`[INFERENCE]` Snowball može pomoći ponašanju, ali može koštati više od highest-effective-cost/avalanche pristupa. Tax, secured, covenant-breaching, personally guaranteed ili operationally critical obligations mogu imati viši prioritet. Plan zahteva rate/fee, maturity, collateral, covenant, liquidity, legal consequence i negotiated-option pregled sa odgovarajućim stručnjakom.

### 10. Expense discipline must protect total-system value

`[FACT — source claim]` Knjiga često traži neposredni 10% cut i znatno višu efficiency, uz brzo ukidanje recurring costs-a (pp. 98–120).

`[INFERENCE]` Arbitrary cut može smanjiti quality, safety, service, retention, capacity, controls ili supplier resilience i time povećati total cost. Svaki cut se testira kroz avoidable cash, customer outcome, gross profit, throughput/constraint, people workload, compliance i reversibility. Card/auto-pay cancellation se radi tek posle contract, credit i continuity provere.

### 11. Customer concentration anecdotes are hypotheses

`[FACT — source claim]` Autor iz consultancy iskustva navodi da top quarter kupaca može davati nesrazmerno više revenue/profit-a, a bottom quarter uništavati profit, pa savetuje fokus na najbolje i uklanjanje loših clients-a (pp. 121–133).

`[INFERENCE]` Ne postoji prikazana metoda koja ove procente čini benchmark-om. Customer decision koristi fulfilled-cohort contribution, CAC, retention, support/returns, payment/default, strategic option, contract/fair-treatment, concentration i replacement capacity — ne etiketu „bad client“.

### 12. Advanced accounts can expose obligations

`[FACT — source content]` Advanced techniques izdvajaju Drip, pass-through/material/subcontractor, sales tax, payroll, employee, equipment, petty cash i vault račune (pp. 152–168).

`[INFERENCE]` Najjači doprinos knjige je vidljivo odvajanje cash-a koji nije slobodno raspoloživ. Customer advance se release-uje preko delivery period-a; sales tax, payroll i pass-through obaveze nisu profit. I dalje su potrebni revenue-recognition, liability, refund, warranty, restricted-cash i reconciliation controls.

### 13. RIFA does not establish market viability

`[FACT — source content]` Reverse-engineered Income For Allocation polazi od željenog personal income-a i vraća se ka potrebnom revenue-u i allocation-u (pp. 152–168).

`[INFERENCE]` Owner need može dati planning constraint, ali ne određuje willingness to pay, addressable demand, capacity, price, cost ili viable business model. Target ostaje `[ASSUMPTION]` dok customer/market evidence i complete economics ne pokažu izvedivost.

### 14. Accountability needs privacy and governance

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga preporučuje accountability groups, zajednički review i u nekim primerima deljenje bank screenshots-a, tax returns-a ili finansijskih podataka (pp. 134–151, 184–192).

`[INFERENCE]` Accountability može ojačati cadence, ali sensitive data se deli samo uz legitimate need, informed consent, minimum scope, secure channel, access/retention controls i conflict/NDA review. Grupa ne zamenjuje accountant-a, fiduciary approval ili internal control.

### 15. People decisions cannot follow magic ratios

`[FACT — source content]` Autor koristi revenue-per-employee/efficiency bands i `A-player` language pri rezovima i reorganizaciji, uz savet da se pravni aspekt proveri i da se pogođenima pomogne (pp. 98–120).

`[INFERENCE]` Firing, pay i workload ne mogu se izvesti iz jednog ratio-a. Potrebni su role/capacity/workload/skill evidence, alternatives, individual due process, equal-treatment i local labor/legal review. Vrednost osobe nije accounting variable ni trajna performance etiketa.

### 16. Personal-finance and family prescriptions are out of scope

`[FACT — source content]` Chapter Ten prenosi sistem na personal/family spending, decu, automobile, lifestyle i recurring family payments (pp. 169–183).

`[INFERENCE]` CMO OS zadržava samo opšti behavioral insight o precommitment-u i named envelopes. Specifične porodične, roditeljske, investment i five-year lifestyle prescription tvrdnje nisu canonical business guidance.

## Sherlock test — limitations and failure modes

1. **Algebra sold as innovation.** `Sales − Profit = Expenses` je ista jednakost; samo behavioral control može stvoriti promenu.
2. **Account-label fallacy.** Cash prebačen u `Profit` account nije dokaz accounting ili economic profit-a.
3. **Cash-source blindness.** Debt, tax, advances, capital i asset-sale cash mogu izgledati kao spendable inflow.
4. **Liability omission.** Envelope balance bez ledger obaveze može potceniti delivery, refund, warranty, payroll, tax ili supplier exposure.
5. **Forecast rejection.** `Bank balance is fact, projection is opinion` može ukloniti forward liquidity control baš kada je najpotrebniji.
6. **Nonstandard denominator.** Real Revenue tretira subcontracted i employed labor nejednako i može proizvesti lažan benchmark.
7. **Target-table authority.** TAP/scale percentages nemaju prikazanu external validation ili industry/jurisdiction calibration.
8. **Public-company analogy.** Net-margin/scale poređenja velikih javnih i malih privatnih firmi nisu like-for-like.
9. **Mandatory distribution.** Quarterly 50% release može podkapitalizovati business ili prekršiti tax, covenant, fiduciary i ownership obaveze.
10. **Reserve conflation.** Profit, distribution, three-month reserve i reinvestment capital imaju različite risk purpose-e.
11. **Absolute no-reinvestment rule.** Profit može legitimno postati retained/reinvestment capital kroz odobrenu capital-allocation odluku.
12. **Calendar error.** Chapter Five navodi `September 31`; septembar ima 30 dana, pa literalni schedule nije pouzdan.
13. **Bill-date rigidity.** 10th/25th rhythm može prekršiti due date, izgubiti discount ili oštetiti supplier relationship.
14. **Blind cancellation.** Ukidanje cards/auto-pay/expenses bez contract i continuity review-a može povećati fee, credit i outage risk.
15. **Debt-snowball cost.** Behavioral win može povećati interest/penalty u odnosu na risk-adjusted priority.
16. **Emergency-credit trap.** Oslanjanje na credit card kao reserve može pogoršati stressed liquidity.
17. **Crude cost cut.** Arbitrary 10% cut ili `double with half` cilj može preneti trošak u failure, burnout ili customer harm.
18. **Anecdotal 80/20.** Customer quartile percentages nemaju dovoljno metodološke potpore za canonical target.
19. **Client disposal bias.** Difficult, low-revenue i unprofitable nisu ista kategorija; contract, fairness i capacity moraju biti provereni.
20. **People-by-ratio.** Revenue-per-head i A-player labels mogu proizvesti unlawful, discriminatory ili system-blind employment decisions.
21. **Account sprawl.** Više banaka/accounts povećava fees, attack surface, fraud, errors i reconciliation workload.
22. **Hidden-account governance.** Uklanjanje online visibility-ja može ukloniti i ovlašćeni oversight/incident detection.
23. **Adviser shopping.** Savet da se accountant zameni ako ospori metod može potisnuti legitimni professional dissent i služi komercijalnom ecosystem-u.
24. **Sensitive-data sharing.** Screenshots/tax returns u peer grupi nose privacy, security, coercion i conflict risk.
25. **Family prescription overreach.** Child, allowance, gift i lifestyle stavovi nisu izvedeni business controls.
26. **Parkinson overreach.** Trošenje ne mora automatski popuniti svaki resource; capacity/safety/resilience slack ponekad je vredan.
27. **Owner-needs fallacy.** Željeni personal income ne dokazuje market demand ili viable price-volume combination.
28. **Profit-as-rainy-day arithmetic.** Procena da određeni allocation procenat daje određen broj nedelja OpEx-a zavisi od stvarnog expense/revenue odnosa i nije univerzalna.

## Cash-allocation decision protocol

### Problem

Definiši konkretan failure: uncontrolled OpEx, late tax, weak owner-pay visibility, debt stress, insufficient reserve ili spending relapse. Named-account metoda nije cilj sama sebi.

### Data

Prikupi reconciled P&L, balance sheet i cash flow; billed/recognized/collected/fulfilled bridge; cash-source breakdown; A/R aging, inventory/service WIP, A/P, tax/payroll/refund/warranty/deferred obligations; debt/covenants; owner role/pay; contractual due dates; forecast, seasonality, access/control map i bank fee/security data. Obeleži evidence status.

### Analysis

1. Odvoji earned profit, cash, restricted/obligated cash, owner compensation, distributable capital i reserve.
2. Napravi minimum 13-week direct cash forecast i downside scenario.
3. Testiraj da li fizički accounts ili sub-ledger daju bolju control/reconciliation ekonomiku.
4. Definiši allocation percentage iz realnih economics-a, ne iz TAP tabele.
5. Testiraj stakeholder, compliance, cyber/fraud i failure-mode posledice.

### Options

Uporedi status quo sa: accounting-only budgets, virtual envelopes/sub-ledger, limited physical accounts, full multi-bank implementation i staged pilot. Za svaku opciju navedi fee, admin, controls, liquidity, reversibility i human-behavior effect.

### Recommendation

Pilotiraj najmanji reversible overlay koji jasno odvaja tax/obligations/reserve i ograničava discretionary OpEx, uz accountant/authority validation. Ne koristi source percentages ili distribution rule dok complete economics i current local obligations nisu potvrđeni.

### Cost

Bank fees, implementation, reconciliation, treasury/admin time, accounting integration, training, access/security, lost discounts, liquidity fragmentation, error/fraud exposure i opportunity cost cash-a.

### Expected result

Očekivani behavioral rezultat je manje neodobrenog discretionary spending-a i bolja vidljivost namene cash-a. Accounting profit, cash runway, debt, taxes i customer outcomes se prognoziraju i mere odvojeno; allocation balance nije outcome.

### KPI

Reconciled operating/net profit; operating cash; minimum cash/runway; tax/payroll/refund/advance coverage; aged payables/receivables; due-date compliance; owner pay versus role benchmark; discretionary OpEx variance; debt cost/covenant headroom; gross profit by fulfilled cohort; bank/reconciliation exceptions; quality/customer/workload/security guardrails.

## Canonical exclusions

- `Sales − Profit = Expenses` nije nova finansijska matematika niti dokaz profitabilnosti.
- Profit account balance nije accounting profit, distributable profit, retained earnings ili free cash flow bez reconciliation-a.
- Current/TAP percentages, scale bands, client quartiles, 10th/25th cadence, quarterly 50% distribution i three-month target nisu Tehnocentar facts ili universal benchmarks.
- Knjiga nije current Serbian accounting, banking, tax, employment, corporate-law ili personal-finance authority.
- Cash envelopes ne zamenjuju P&L, balance sheet, cash-flow statement, forecast, tax/payroll/debt controls, accountant-a, audit ili approval rights.
- Firing, pay cuts, workload i customer termination se ne izvode iz revenue-per-head, account balance-a ili A/B/C etikete.
- Sensitive financial data se ne deli sa accountability grupom bez legitimate purpose-a, consent-a i security/governance controls-a.
- Family/child/lifestyle prescriptions, practitioner marketing i absolute success promises nisu canonical CMO OS knowledge.
