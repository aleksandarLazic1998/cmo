## Central thesis

`[FACT — source claim]` Financial statements nisu sirova stvarnost. Income statement i balance sheet sadrže pravila, procene, klasifikacije i vremenske odluke; cash flow je neposredniji, ali ni on sam ne objašnjava održivost poslovanja. Financially intelligent manager razume definitions, povezuje sva tri statements-a, ispituje assumptions i koristi ratios/ROI/working-capital levers u context-u.

`[INFERENCE]` CMO OS prevodi tezu u `financial reconciliation discipline`: nijedna komercijalna preporuka ne prolazi samo na revenue-u, profit-u, bank balance-u, margin-u, ROI procentu ili jednom ratio-u. Mora pomiriti izvor broja, accounting treatment, cash timing, balance-sheet obavezu, unit economics, capacity, downside i current jurisdictional policy.

## Complete financial intelligence model

### 1. The accounting-art lens

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga razlikuje legalnu računovodstvenu diskreciju od prevare i stalno pita koje assumptions, estimates i biases stoje iza broja: recognition, allocation, useful life, reserve, valuation, capitalization ili one-time classification (pp. 12–33).

`[INFERENCE]` Broj dobija decision weight tek kada su poznati definition, entity, period, unit, accounting basis, source system, owner, last close/reconciliation, estimate sensitivity i comparison baseline. `Estimate` ne znači proizvoljno; zahteva policy, evidence, consistency, controls i professional review.

### 2. Income statement as period economics

`[FACT — source content]` Revenue se priznaje kada je earned prema primenjenim pravilima, expenses se matching-om vezuju za period, a gross/operating/net profit odgovaraju na različita pitanja (pp. 36–69).

`[INFERENCE]` CMO recommendation mora navesti tačan profit layer. Gross profit ne pokriva automatski operating expense, tax, financing, replacement capex ili owner distribution. Revenue target bez contribution/gross margin, fulfillment cost i recognized-versus-billed-versus-collected razlike je nepotpun.

### 3. Recognition and classification control

`[FACT — source content]` Long projects, bundled contracts, bill-and-hold, pass-through costs, COGS/COS granica, depreciation life, amortization i restructuring charges menjaju prikaz bez nužne promene operativne stvarnosti (pp. 49–64).

`[INFERENCE]` Pre bonusa, trend zaključka ili experiment outcome-a proveri da li se definition/policy promenila. Restatement na prethodnu osnovu ili bridge analysis odvaja operating change od accounting presentation change-a.

### 4. Balance sheet as cumulative position

`[FACT — source content]` Balance sheet prikazuje assets, liabilities i equity na određeni datum, a fundamental equation održava vezu između onoga što business kontroliše i izvora finansiranja (pp. 73–103).

`[INFERENCE]` Asset nije isto što i ready cash, book value nije market value, a positive equity nije dokaz likvidnosti. A/R quality, inventory obsolescence, debt maturity, tax/refund/warranty/deferred-revenue obligations, restricted cash i off-balance/contingent exposure moraju se proveriti prema aktuelnim pravilima.

### 5. Three-statement reconciliation

`[FACT — source content]` Credit sale utiče na revenue i A/R pre cash-a; inventory purchase i sale prelaze kroz balance sheet i COGS; net profit ulazi u retained earnings; operating cash se reconciles od profit-a kroz noncash items i working-capital promene (pp. 97–128).

`[INFERENCE]` Svaki scenario dobija minimalni bridge: `recognized revenue → gross/operating profit → working-capital movement → operating cash → capex/financing → ending cash → remaining obligations`. Ako se bridge ne zatvara, odluka ostaje `[UNKNOWN]`.

### 6. Profit and cash are jointly necessary

`[FACT — source content]` Growing profitable business može propasti kada plaća dobavljače/zaposlene pre naplate kupaca; unprofitable cash business može privremeno izgledati zdravo zbog float-a (pp. 110–116).

`[INFERENCE]` Rast može povećati funding gap. Zato se plan rasta testira kroz receivable terms, inventory/service WIP, payable terms, tax timing, refunds, payroll, capex, minimum cash/runway i downside collection scenario — ne samo kroz P&L.

### 7. Cash-flow-source quality

`[FACT — source content]` Cash-flow statement odvaja operations, investing i financing. Pozitivan net cash može dolaziti iz operations-a, asset sale-a, novog debt-a ili equity-ja, što nosi različito značenje (pp. 117–134).

`[INFERENCE]` `Cash is hard to fudge` nije dozvoljen apsolut. Payment deferral, collection acceleration, factoring, classification, supplier pressure i one-off financing mogu popraviti period bez trajne ekonomike. Analiza traži multi-period trend i source/uses decomposition.

### 8. Ratios as contextual questions

`[FACT — source content]` Profitability, leverage, liquidity i efficiency ratios služe za trend, plan i peer comparison; definicije mogu varirati i svaki ratio nasleđuje slabosti input-a (pp. 135–163).

`[INFERENCE]` Ratio nema univerzalni `good` broj bez business model-a, seasonality-ja, accounting policy-ja i distribution-a. Koristi average denominators gde je potrebno, proveri aging/tails iza averages-a i upari svaki target sa counter-metric-om da ne izazove gaming.

### 9. Profitability and asset productivity

`[FACT — source content]` Gross/operating/net margin mere različite slojeve, ROA povezuje profit i assets, ROE profit i equity, a DuPont veza pokazuje da ROA može rasti margin-om ili turnover-om (pp. 142–147, 161–163).

`[INFERENCE]` Visok ROE može poticati iz leverage-a, visok ROA iz nedovoljnog reinvestiranja ili malog/iskrivljenog asset base-a. Zato se return ratio razlaže na driver-e, upoređuje sa cost/risk of capital i proverava održivost asset/capacity baze.

### 10. Liquidity and leverage

`[FACT — source content]` Debt-to-equity, interest coverage, current ratio i quick ratio postavljaju pitanja o financing risk-u i sposobnosti plaćanja (pp. 148–154).

`[INFERENCE]` Threshold `1` ili bilo koji isolated covenant nije univerzalni health test. Cash forecast, committed facilities, maturity schedule, covenant headroom, customer concentration, receivable collectability, inventory liquidity i stress scenario imaju prednost. Historical operating-lease treatment iz knjige nije current policy.

### 11. Working capital and cash conversion

`[FACT — source content]` DSO, DII, DPO i CCC povezuju prodaju, inventory/service cycle, naplatu i dobavljače; smanjenje receivables/inventory može osloboditi cash bez promene revenue-a (pp. 155–160, 184–197).

`[INFERENCE]` Working-capital optimizacija ne znači automatski `minimum inventory`, `maximum DPO` ili najstroži credit. Guardrails su stockout/service level, defects, lost sales, supplier resilience, fair terms, complaints, bad debt i concentration. Aging/distribution imaju prednost nad samim average-om.

`[INFERENCE]` Knjiška procena `sales/day × CCC` nije pouzdana zamena za stvarni net working-capital requirement jer DII/DPO koriste COGS, DSO koristi sales, a margins, taxes i ostale current stavke nisu pomirene. Koristi direct cash forecast i balance-sheet bridge.

### 12. ROI as cash-flow decision model

`[FACT — source content]` Capital analysis traži initial cash outlay, future incremental cash flows i hurdle rate; payback daje recovery speed, NPV dollar value iznad required return-a, a IRR implicitni percentage return (pp. 164–183).

`[INFERENCE]` NPV je primarni comparison signal kada su cash flows i discount rate adekvatni, ali nije dokaz sam po sebi. Potrebni su incremental counterfactual, cannibalization, ramp, maintenance, working capital, tax, terminal value/decommissioning, execution capacity, option value, risk-adjusted scenarios i post-investment review.

### 13. Hurdle rate and sensitivity

`[FACT — source content]` Required return treba da nadmaši cost of capital i odrazi risk/opportunity cost; NPV pada kada discount rate raste (pp. 164–181).

`[INFERENCE]` Jedan arbitrary hurdle rate može previše odbaciti long-term capability ili prihvatiti loše procenjen cash flow. Odluka prikazuje base/upside/downside, sensitivity ključnih driver-a, break-even, probability ako je opravdana i strategic/nonfinancial consequences odvojeno od financial estimate-a.

### 14. Financial literacy and transparency

`[FACT — source content]` Autori preporučuju ponovljenu kratku obuku, relevantne timske brojeve, scoreboards i objašnjenje finansijske logike kako bi ljudi donosili bolje lokalne odluke (pp. 198–212).

`[INFERENCE]` Transparency nije objavljivanje svega svima. Potrebni su legitimate purpose, access control, confidentiality, privacy, inside-information/security, context i right-to-question. KPI literacy ne sme postati target pressure, surveillance ili prebacivanje system failure-a na radnika.

## Sherlock test — limitations and failure modes

1. **Art becomes relativism.** To što accounting uključuje judgement ne znači da je svaka procena dozvoljena; policy, standard, evidence, controls i audit ostaju obavezni.
2. **Historical-rule drift.** 2006 U.S. GAAP, lease, goodwill, R&D, extraordinary-item i Sarbanes–Oxley objašnjenja nisu current Serbian/IFRS policy.
3. **Cash absolutism.** Cash je manje estimate-sensitive, ali period cash može biti window-dressed payment timing-om, factoring-om, financing-om ili supplier pressure-om.
4. **EBIT/EBITDA simplification.** Ignorisanje financing, tax, depreciation ili replacement capex-a može sakriti asset intensity, debt risk i stvarnu reinvestment potrebu.
5. **Free-cash-flow ambiguity.** `Operating cash − capex` ne razdvaja maintenance/growth capex, leases, acquisition, working-capital normalization ili druge owner obligations.
6. **Book value confusion.** Positive assets/equity mogu sadržati uncollectible A/R, obsolete inventory, impaired goodwill ili undervalued/overvalued items.
7. **Ratio threshold mythology.** Current/quick ratio iznad 1, visok margin ili visok turnover nisu univerzalni success standard.
8. **Average hides tail.** DSO, inventory days i average margins mogu sakriti mali broj veoma starih receivables, dead stock ili loss-making cohorts.
9. **Denominator distortion.** ROA/ROE sa ending balance-om, sezonskim stanjem ili accounting change-om može dati lažan improvement.
10. **Leverage sugar high.** Visok ROE uz više debt-a povećava downside i covenant/liquidity risk.
11. **Working-capital local optimum.** Manji inventory može izazvati stockout; duži DPO može uništiti dobavljača; stroži credit može izgubiti profitabilnog kupca.
12. **CCC cash overstatement.** `Sales/day × CCC` meša sales i COGS basis i nije direct cash requirement.
13. **Capitalization gaming.** Expense-to-asset klasifikacija popravlja kratkoročni profit, ali dodaje buduću amortization/impairment obavezu i audit risk.
14. **One-time normalization abuse.** Ponavljani restructuring ili “extraordinary” trošak nije automatski non-operating niti beznačajan.
15. **Revenue gaming.** Bill-and-hold, channel stuffing, bundled allocation i milestone judgement mogu platiti bonus bez realne cash/customer vrednosti.
16. **NPV precision theater.** Tačan decimalni rezultat nad slabim cash-flow assumptions daje lažnu sigurnost.
17. **IRR selection error.** IRR može pogrešno rangirati mutually exclusive projekte različite skale, trajanja ili cash-flow pattern-a.
18. **Payback myopia.** Brz povrat ignoriše cash nakon cutoff-a, time value i long-term strategic value.
19. **Hurdle-rate bias.** Uniforman visok hurdle favorizuje kratke projekte i može potisnuti safety, compliance ili capability ulaganja koja nisu optional.
20. **Forecast omission.** P&L/ratio bez rolling cash forecast-a ne hvata payroll, tax, debt maturity i seasonal minimum cash.
21. **Transparency overreach.** Finansijska otvorenost bez access/privacy/security kontrole može naneti štetu i prekršiti obaveze.
22. **KPI gaming.** Commission on collection, DSO, unit cost, margin ili inventory target može prebaciti štetu na kupca, dobavljača, kvalitet ili zaposlene.
23. **Training causality overclaim.** Association između literacy programa i performance-a ne dokazuje da je training uzrok.
24. **Authority displacement.** Financial literacy ne ovlašćuje nefinansijskog manager-a da zaobiđe accountant-a, auditor-a, legal-a, tax professional-a ili formalne controls.

## Financial decision protocol

### Problem

Imenuj decision, owner, deadline, relevant entity/segment/cohort i failure koji pokušavaš da sprečiš. Navedi koji finansijski broj izgleda problematično i zašto sam po sebi nije dovoljan.

### Data

Prikupi source statements, chart-of-accounts/policy definition, period/units, actual-versus-budget/prior, billing/collection, A/R aging, inventory/service WIP, A/P, tax/refund/warranty/deferred obligations, debt/covenants, capex, cash forecast, customer outcome, capacity i risk. Obeleži `[FACT]`, `[INFERENCE]`, `[ASSUMPTION]` i `[UNKNOWN]`.

### Analysis

1. Reconcile P&L, balance sheet i cash flow.
2. Napravi bridge od revenue-a do ending cash-a i remaining obligations.
3. Razloži ratio/variance na operational driver, accounting change i timing.
4. Prikaži base/downside/upside i sensitivity ključnih assumptions.
5. Proveri system effects na customer, supplier, people, quality, compliance i resilience.

### Options

Za svaku opciju navedi incremental cash flows, P&L/balance-sheet treatment, working-capital effect, capital/capacity need, reversibility, risk i do-nothing baseline. Ne sabiraj međusobno isključive benefits i ne sakrivaj transfer štete.

### Recommendation

Izaberi opciju koja najviše povećava risk-adjusted, sustainable customer/economic value uz authority i evidence. Ako accounting/tax/legal treatment ili material inputs nisu validirani, preporuka je conditional ili `STOP`, ne finalna tvrdnja.

### Cost

Uključi acquisition/build, implementation, training, downtime, working capital, financing, tax, maintenance, support, replacement/decommissioning, opportunity cost i downside reserve.

### Expected result

Odvoji expected operating outcome, recognized profit, cash timing, balance-sheet effect i uncertainty interval. Navedi kada će se rezultat pojaviti i koji leading evidence mora prethoditi.

### KPI

Koristi mali set povezanih indikatora: qualified/fulfilled revenue, gross/operating profit, operating/free cash with definition, A/R aging/DSO, inventory or service WIP/DII, DPO/supplier health, CCC, minimum cash/runway, ROIC/NPV realization, customer outcome, quality, workload/safety i risk/compliance. Svaki KPI ima definition, owner, source, cadence, counter-metric i review trigger.
