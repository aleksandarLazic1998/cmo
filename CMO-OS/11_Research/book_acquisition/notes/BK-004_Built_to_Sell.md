## Central thesis

`[FACT — source claim]` Firma postaje vredniji i slobodniji asset kada kupci ne zavise od vlasnika, kada zaposleni mogu ponovljivo prodati i isporučiti standardizovanu ponudu, kada postoji management layer i kada prihod/cash nisu zavisni od jednog kupca ili jednog rainmaker-a.

`[INFERENCE]` `Built to sell` je bolje čitati kao `built to operate without founder dependency`. Prodaja firme je samo jedna opcija; isti sistem može povećati otpornost, delegiranje i strategic freedom i kada se firma ne prodaje.

## Full model

### The dependency trap

`[FACT — source content]` Početni Stapleton Agency zavisi od Alex-a za sales, client management, judgment i kvalitet; najveći kupac nosi 40% revenue-a, tim su generalisti, ponude se customizuju, a spor billing stvara negative cash cycle (pp. 17–31).

`[INFERENCE]` Owner dependency postoji kada materijalni outcome nema alternativnog odgovornog, standard, decision right ili evidence trail. Ne meri se time koliko je vlasnik zauzet, već šta bi stalo ili se pogoršalo kada je odsutan.

### Scalable-offer gate

`[FACT — source content]` Implementation guide definiše tri kriterijuma (pp. 114–120):

1. `Teachable` — zaposleni ili tehnologija mogu dosledno isporučiti.
2. `Valuable` — rešava značajan customer problem dovoljno dobro da izbegne čistu komoditizaciju.
3. `Repeatable` — kupac ili customer cohort ima razlog za ponovnu kupovinu/prihod.

`[INFERENCE]` CMO OS dodaje četvrti kriterijum: `profit-bearing`. Ponuda mora stvarati održiv gross profit i cash posle CAC-a, delivery/support troška, refund/default rizika, capacity potrošnje i potrebnih investicija.

### Specialization and productized process

`[FACT — source content]` Priča standardizuje pet koraka logo-dizajna, imenuje proces, dokumentuje tačna uputstva, postavlja flat price i odbija rad van ponude. Cilj je da druga osoba može prodati i isporučiti istu logiku bez vlasnika (pp. 27–69).

`[INFERENCE]` Standardizacija treba da definiše outcome, ulaze, granice, quality checks, exception path i owner-a. Slepo ponavljanje bez exception governance-a može samo skalirati grešku.

### Customer concentration

`[FACT — source claim]` Knjiga koristi pravilo da nijedan kupac ne treba da čini više od 15% revenue-a (pp. 31–32).

`[INFERENCE]` Prag je heuristic. Relevantan concentration risk zavisi od gross profit contribution-a, payment behavior-a, contractual duration-a, switching/replacement time-a, common-cause exposure-a, product concentration-a i cash runway-a. Mora se scenario-testirati, ne mehanički primeniti.

### Cash conversion and working capital

`[FACT — source content]` Advance/progress billing skraćuje cash conversion cycle i može smanjiti working-capital funding potrebu; purchase price nije dovoljan bez razumevanja working-capital adjustment-a (pp. 36–39, 121–123).

`[INFERENCE]` Upfront cash nije automatski profit ni slobodan novac. Može biti deferred-revenue obaveza uz budući delivery, refund, warranty, tax/VAT i capacity commitment. CMO OS prati P&L, balance sheet i cash flow zajedno.

### Repeatable revenue hierarchy

`[FACT — source content]` Autor rangira consumables, sunk-money consumables, renewable subscriptions, sunk-money subscriptions, auto-renew subscriptions i term contracts od manje ka više vrednim (pp. 116–120).

`[INFERENCE]` Contract form sam ne određuje kvalitet prihoda. Potrebni su gross retention, net retention, churn/cancellation rights, cohort margin, collection, servicing obligation, discount, customer concentration, compliance i reputation risk. Auto-renewal bez transparentnosti može stvarati pravni i trust problem.

### Sales engine independent of the founder

`[FACT — source content]` Pipeline se prevodi u leads → appointments → closes → revenue model; dve sales reps treba da pokažu da rezultat nije samo founder ili superstar effect (pp. 51–60).

`[INFERENCE]` Dve osobe ne dokazuju skalabilnost. Potrebni su cohort ramp time, source mix, conversion confidence interval, sales-cycle distribution, quota attainment, CAC, discount, churn i capacity constraint. Product-selling naspram service-selling profila je context heuristic, ne univerzalno hiring pravilo.

### Management and incentives

`[FACT — source content]` Firma gradi management layer za sales, client service i creative delivery. Knjiga preferira deferred cash incentive/stay bonus umesto equity-ja kako bi smanjila transaction complexity i retention risk (pp. 72–75, 97–100, 133–135).

`[INFERENCE]` Incentive mora uskladiti profitabilan long-term outcome, customer quality, controls i ethical behavior. Sam profit share može izazvati short-term optimization; sam stay bonus može zadržati pogrešnu osobu. Equity, cash i hybrid izbor zavise od prava, poreza, capital plan-a i potreba tima.

### Sale-readiness and transaction process

`[FACT — source content]` Knjiga mapira: adviser selection → teaser → NDA → Book/data room → management presentation → LOI → exclusivity → due diligence → revised/binding agreement → working-capital adjustment → closing (pp. 82–113, 130–138).

`[INFERENCE]` Posrednik ima potencijalni conflict jer zarađuje ako deal zatvori i može imati jači odnos sa buyer-om. Potrebni su written mandate, conflict disclosure, buyer competition, independent legal/tax/accounting advice i decision authority vlasnika.

`[FACT — source content]` LOI je prikazan kao nonbinding i podložan re-trade-u; earn-out treba tretirati kao rizičan, a due diligence proverava dokumente, customer contracts, IP, technology licenses, credit covenants, receivables, permits i litigation (pp. 106–138).

## Moriarty analysis — what survives

- `[INFERENCE]` Mapiraj founder dependency kao single points of failure u sales-u, delivery-ju, decision-making-u i trust-u.
- `[INFERENCE]` Standardizuj najpre posao koji je teachable, valuable, repeatable i profit-bearing; ne standardizuj activity bez customer/economic outcome-a.
- `[INFERENCE]` Pozitivan cash cycle može finansirati rast, ali deferred obligations ostaju zaštićene.
- `[INFERENCE]` Ne dozvoli da jedan buyer, employee, channel, supplier ili founder bude nevidljivi veto nad sistemom.
- `[INFERENCE]` Sale-readiness je evidence discipline: ugovori, IP, licence, receivables, procesi, KPI i ownership moraju biti dostupni i dosledni pre due diligence-a.

## Sherlock test — limitations and failure modes

1. **Single-offer risk.** Specijalizacija u jednom offer-u smanjuje complexity, ali povećava product/market/regulatory concentration.
2. **Customer-blind standardization.** Odbijanje svake varijacije može propustiti profitabilan segment ili važan learning signal.
3. **False recurrence.** Povremena ponovna kupovina nije contractually ili behaviorally predictable recurring revenue.
4. **Cash illusion.** Advance payment može izgledati kao excess cash iako finansira buduću isporuku.
5. **P&L dismissal.** Savet da se „ignoriše P&L“ je opasan ako se čita bukvalno; timing razlika se reconciliuje, a profitabilnost i balance sheet ostaju obavezni.
6. **Pipeline overfit.** Dva zatvorena posla iz warm lead baze nisu pouzdan cold-market conversion estimate.
7. **Market-size fiction.** Broj firmi × observed close rate × cena bez reachability, frequency, competition, capacity i uncertainty nije dokaz TAM-a.
8. **Quality drift.** Instruction manual i junior delivery mogu smanjiti tacit expertise i customer outcome ako nema QA i escalation-a.
9. **Incentive gaming.** Sales competition, bonus pool i deal bonus mogu povećati low-quality sales, concealment ili short-term behavior bez guardrails-a.
10. **Owner withdrawal too early.** Uklanjanje founder-a pre prenosa judgment-a može samo sakriti dependency.
11. **Buyer dependence.** Earn-out outcome može zavisiti od buyer allocation-a, accounting policies, integration decisions i promene kontrole.
12. **Advice drift.** Legal, tax, accounting, noncompete, auto-renewal i transaction practice iz 2010/2011 ne primenjuju se bez current professional validation.
13. **Ethical framing.** Buyer-facing story ne sme skrivati stvarni razlog prodaje ili materijalne rizike; persuasive framing ostaje istinit i complete.

## CMO OS founder-independence protocol

### Problem

Identifikuj gde owner absence zaustavlja sales, delivery, approval, relationship ili cash collection. Navedi economic consequence i time-to-failure.

### Data

Za svaku zavisnost prikupi process map, responsible role, decision rights, cycle time, quality/rework, customer concentration, contribution margin, cash timing i exception frequency.

### Analysis

Oceni ponudu kroz `teachable × valuable × repeatable × profit-bearing`. Razdvoji standard case, allowed variation i escalation. Scenario-testiraj gubitak owner-a, najvećeg kupca, ključnog zaposlenog i channel-a.

### Options

Uporedi dokumentovanje, training/backup, automation, specialization, offer modularization, management hire i kontrolisano ukidanje custom rada. Za svaku opciju navedi cost, time, risk i expected operating effect.

### Recommendation

Ukloni najmaterijalniji single point of failure koji ima dokaziv customer/economic benefit, uz QA i cash guardrails. Ne širi offer dok current delivery nije repeatable.

### KPI

- founder-involved revenue share;
- customer concentration po revenue-u i gross profit-u;
- on-time/first-pass delivery;
- exception/rework rate;
- gross margin by offer/cohort;
- lead-to-win, CAC/payback i sales ramp;
- deferred revenue versus delivery obligation;
- cash conversion cycle;
- management coverage i documented decision rights.
