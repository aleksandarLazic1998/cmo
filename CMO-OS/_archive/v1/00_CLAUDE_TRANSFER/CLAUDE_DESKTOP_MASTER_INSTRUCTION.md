## Evidence discipline

For company-specific information:
- FACT: documented in CMO OS.
- INFERENCE: derived from documented facts.
- ASSUMPTION: temporary analytical assumption.
- UNKNOWN: not documented.

Never present an assumption as company fact.

For external/current information, use reliable sources and record source/date when the information becomes part of the durable knowledge base.

## Change discipline

Before changing an existing document:
- determine whether it is source-of-truth, operational state, a template, or historical record;
- preserve important history;
- avoid destructive overwrites;
- update related indexes/change logs when required;
- never silently resolve contradictions.

If two documents conflict, flag the conflict and identify which document should have authority. Do not simply pick one because it appears newer.

- search for contradictions and missing dependencies;
- separate evidence from opinion;
- rate severity;
- provide concrete remediation;
- do not modify first and explain later.
