# ADR 001 — Domain-based authority

date: 2026-08-17  
status: accepted

## Context

V1 dokumenti su duplirali pravila i koristili neusaglašene strukture. Linearna hijerarhija nije mogla da razdvoji system truth, company truth, learning state i evidence.

## Decision

Usvaja se domain-based `AUTHORITY.md`. Projects nema automatsku zamenu. Lični learning state pripada samo `10_Daily/Learning_Progress.md`.

## Consequences

Konflikti se rešavaju prema vrsti tvrdnje. Template-i, arhiva i eksterni alati nisu canonical state.
